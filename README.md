## What this is

`learnix` documents the resources I use to learn: courses, books, docs, hands-on labs, and what I actually took away from each. Part study log, part honest resource review, part certification tracker.

If you're learning the same tools, treat this as a map: what's worth your time, what to skip, and the notes that made a concept finally click.

## What you'll find

| Section | What's inside |
|---------|---------------|
| **[Topics](topics/)** | Notes organized by subject: the concepts, distilled into my own words. |
| **[Certifications](certifications/)** | Study plans, practice logs, and post-exam retros for the certs I chase. |
| **[Resource ledger](resources/)** | Every course and book I've used, with an honest rating and whether it was worth it. |
| **[Learning log](log/)** | An optional running timeline of what I'm working through. |

## How it's organized

Everything is anchored to **topics** (the backbone), with resources and timeline as supporting views. Notes start as rough seeds and get promoted to `evergreen` as they mature. The durable, production-ready operational bits eventually graduate into my private ops journal, not published here.
